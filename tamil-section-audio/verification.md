# Tamil section audio verification

The published iOS update exported 72 assets, including five offline MP3s. The previous export contained 953 assets, including 886 standalone MP3s.

The importer regenerated byte-identical section tracks and metadata from the approved source deck. An independent check decoded all 1,014 original recordings and compared each one to the indexed interval in the generated MP3. Minimum waveform correlation was 0.9858. The check also measured silence between all indexed phrases.

In Expo Go on the tamil-audio iOS simulator, the Colors listening exercise played the same phrase at both speeds. The simulator process audio was captured separately from the screen. The original audible speech lasted 1.64 seconds. Measured speech lasted 1.64 seconds at 1× and 2.18 seconds at 0.75×. The spectral envelopes correlated 0.995 at each speed. Silence followed each phrase. [Watch with audio](normal-and-slow.mp4).
