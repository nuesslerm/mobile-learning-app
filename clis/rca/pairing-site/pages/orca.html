Pair Orca Mobile

Use a private link to connect Orca to RCA on your Mac. Install Orca Mobile and connect to Tailscale on this iPhone first.

  1. Create a pairing link

    Create the link on the iPhone you want to pair.

  2. Confirm in Orca

    Tap Open in Orca. When Orca asks Pair with this desktop?, tap Pair.

  3. Open your Mac in Orca

    Go to Feed → Desktops → Mac · Mobile Learning, then choose a worktree. The workspace interface may take a moment to download on first open.
