Original decision-log entry
- 13 Nov, voice-edit e2e and cache audit, evidence. (1) The voice-edit failures had two causes. Planner HTTP 500 (turns 1 to 3, "no plan.response"): the planner retry of e01327cb only reached prod with rca 3ed11cc2 (run 43502172560 runs its e2e before its own deploy, so it failed on both flavors); in the next run (43508747174) the browser e2e passed. Speech-to-text (turn 4 on Android, "no transcribe.response"): the speech adapter had no retry; one retry for a transport failure, 5xx or 429 landed in 0c2f1e3 (flow tests: retried once then used; a 400 is not retried). (2) Cache audit of runs 43468878073 to 43496297726 (read-only, five layers): the bucket holds 8,100 `nx/` objects back to 30 Oct and `meta/generation` is unchanged since 26 Oct, so the "expired" Nx verdicts of the cold run 43488019983 were its own `[cache:no]`, not eviction. Real findings: sccache was never on PATH in the 12 cargo test jobs (wired in 90e6081), the storage guard was not started (wired in 90e6081), Dockerfile and compose changes reran typecheck/lint/test:unit of two packages (91 s, c7def95), `wf ci live` held a step for 60 s (5fe7421: 15 s bound), the metrics flush budget of 2 s dropped readings (3ed11cc: 6 s). (3) Run 43515968628: two jobs failed on `error: no matching package named rca-core found, location searched: mla index` (flow-tests 5/6 and integration-tests 4/6, within seconds of each other, no library publish in that run, the same index GET answers 200 minutes later): a transient answer from the public libs-index bucket that cargo does not retry on a "not found"; watch, and if it recurs vendor or mirror the index into the build.
