Original decision-log entry
- 21 Nov, issue #83 Q1, Q2 and P1 leftovers: the browser e2e Chromium and its `.deb` files cached, Android shard weights, e2e rows reaching the CI database, sccache saved per shard (test type: flow tests over the workflow and action text and the deal itself, `cargo test -p pipeline`, n=1; no hosted run was read, so no seconds are claimed). (1) Playwright: `install-test-deps` already restored `~/.cache/ms-playwright` and `~/playwright-debs`; the browser e2e job did not and ran an unbounded `install --with-deps`. The cache steps and the install are now `playwright-cache` (action and `install.sh`), used by both. (2) Android: the five hosted two-shard runs in `CI_STEPS` give the shard with tamil-writing and editor-typing a median 319 s `Run the e2e tests` against 244 s for the other, while the weights summed to 217 s and 221 s; the weights are scaled by those two ratios and the deal moves editor-typing to the voice-edit shard (297 s against 266 s predicted). Decision: keep, with the figures and their limits (n=5, retries inside, no per-test row) in `shard-weights-and-overlapping-runs.md`. (3) The e2e jobs never ran the `hosted-stats` upload, so `CI_UNITS` and `NX_TASKS` held no browser or Android row; the upload is added, which is also why the weights could not be regenerated for them. (4) sccache: the 20 Nov entry above chose one writer per stage because the shards compile the same dependencies; the issue lists per-shard saves as the open item, and the issue lists per-shard saves as the open item, so the key now names the shard and each shard saves its own; the premise that the shards build different workspace crates (and the 20 Nov premise that they build the same ones) is not checked against hit rates. Reverses that entry; the cost (22 entries against the 10 GB quota, about 6 GB at the measured 268 MB each, and LRU eviction of nx, metro and playwright entries when it is full) is unmeasured and documented in `cache-layers.md`, with the revert rule (lower `max-mb` or one saving shard per stage) if the job summaries show those layers missing. (5) The 13-minute EAS publish: not found. `CI_JOBS` holds 17 `eas-update` runs of 19 to 21 Nov, the longest 300 s with the step `Publish the EAS Update` at most 186 s, and 36 hosted runs read through the API show none above 300 s; the 20 Nov run is not in the database, so no cause is claimed and no code was changed for it.
