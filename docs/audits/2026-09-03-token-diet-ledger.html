1. Where the money goes

One remote session, 4,231 API calls over 524 turns, read from its own transcript. Median context per call 442 K tokens, p90 695 K; 74% of calls ran with more than 300 K in the window. Tool results totalled 7.5 MB and were re-read on every later call.

cache reads1,839 M81%
cache writes22.1 M12%
output (incl. thinking)3.15 M7%
uncached input0.2 M0%

So terse prose can move at most the 7%. Fewer tokens per call, fewer calls per turn and a smaller fixed prefix (about 50 K tokens per session start) move the rest. Independent benchmarks agree: JetBrains measured caveman at −8.5% output tokens (advertised −65%), rtk at +7.6% cost, and Max Taylor found "Be brief." ties caveman on 24 chat prompts.

Does it hold over a conversation?

Reading. The plan step costs about $0.26 of Sonnet whatever the task size ($0.257, $0.263, $0.260), and the implementation $0.004–$0.021 of opencode. The headless tree therefore loses on small (2.7×) and medium (1.5×) tasks and wins on the large one (−54%). The crossover sits where Sonnet alone would spend more than about $0.30. Below it, the whole task on opencode (arm W, $0.004–$0.026, 7/7 gates) or Sonnet alone is cheaper; the plan's adoption rule (≥40% saved on ≥5/7 tasks) is not met for small and medium work. n=1 per task.

Where the tree earns its keep is the interactive flow measured live on a real feature (section 6): Claude spends three short documents and one adjudication, and the implementation, research, review and documentation cost about $0.07 of opencode.

Two harness bugs surfaced and were fixed the same hour: the model rotation overrode an agent's pinned model after a timeout (the review ran on MiniMax), and a read-only agent narrated "I wrote the draft" instead of printing it, so the six-line narration was saved as the document. opencodeDispatch now takes models, and runProse rejects a reply that is not the document and asks once more.

7. What is on now

  • Output style token-diet from .agents/config/claude-settings.json: at most 3 lines, bullets, answer first, no code unless asked, 🪨 canary on every reply.
  • Prefix diet: 26 skills removed (caveman among them), CLAUDE.md 11.7 → 5.9 KB, workflow-cli gated to frontend/**, agentation MCP off by default.
  • Quiet hooks: nothing printed on a healthy turn; two Stop hooks that had crashed on every turn fixed.
  • Tool output on a diet: rtk 0.47.0 installed by env-bootstrap run --step rtk (pinned, checksummed) behind the PreToolUse Bash hook; Bash|Read results over 800 B enter context as a head plus a pointer.
  • Delegation: the L3 reminder, /cheap, and the cheap-flow plugin (/cf-brainstorm → /cf-plan → /cf-work → /cf-review → /cf-compound) on three opencode agents pinned to deepseek-v4-flash; wf opencode savings prices runs with the B1 ratios.

8. Caveats

  • B2 is n=1 per task; identical runs vary by about 20%. B1 is n=3.
  • The judge is a cheap model scoring a rubric; it caught dropped facts, not subtle quality loss.
  • Eagerness cells used a replay stub for opencode; the eagerness numbers measure routing, not delivered work.
  • rtk's saving here is chat-shaped (−19%) and near neutral on agentic tasks (−4%); JetBrains' +7.6% cost was not reproduced, but the agentic effect is within run-to-run variance.
  • The Guard e2e CI job on PR #236 is red for a missing ANTHROPIC_API_KEY (OAuth-only policy), unrelated to any of this.
