# F2. Cursor navigation and cold start: the plan of item F2 of issue 86

Planned 22 Nov 2026 by a read-only reader of the code, checked against the issue (Decisions 1 to 47, sections A to E). The issue body (#86) holds the decisions and the item state; this file holds the slice-level plan: files, tests and what proves each slice. Shorthand: `L/` is `frontend/mla-tamagui/features/leetcode/`.

## Summary

Cursor: a fixed 56-wide pad at the left end of the action rail (outside the rail's horizontal scroll, built with RNGH Pan like the Tamil canvas), behind a setting that is off until the owner has used it. A drag maps the finger's displacement from where it grabbed to a caret target. There is no velocity and no clock: sideways is characters for the first 12 characters of travel and words after that, vertical is lines with a goal column, the axis locks after a few px, and with a selection at the grab the head moves and the anchor stays. Every motion is a pure function in `domain/editor` over the editor's own text, read once with `getText` at the grab (the React copy and `lastWebViewTextRef` can be a keystroke behind and lose the anchor and revision, decision 29). A latest-wins coalescer gated on the reply, not on a frame timer, keeps one `select` in flight carrying the newest target, so there is never a backlog and nothing waits on a clock. The `select` step gains `reveal` so the caret scrolls into view without a second call (`view.reveal` centers the line, which suits E2's jump and not a drag); that is the only library change and the only generated-file change on the cursor side. Cold start: first measure with one `editor.cold_start` event whose durations come from an injected monotonic clock (the console echo is buffered about 1 s and carries no time, so a log's own timestamp is useless), a page-side `evalMs` carried on `ready` (the hand-written `editorHtml.ts`, no regeneration), and a deterministic byte budget on `cm6Bundle.ts` (now 662,693 bytes). The trims are conditional on those numbers. The runner warm-up gate is the likely big one, because the unconfirmed 8.4 s stalls in `docs/gaps/android-pyodide-warmup-may-stall-editor-events.md` dwarf anything in the bundle. Lazy grammar init through `require` inside the one bundle is the safe bundle trim. Rejected: a second script for the language packs (it would load a second copy of CodeMirror state, whose extensions the first copy rejects, or force the page to export whole module namespaces and grow), an editor preloaded at the app root (a WebView cannot be re-parented, and its layout would sit above the editor zone, decision 18), a static code preview before hydration (it looks tappable and is not), and hydrating before the problem text arrives (the catalog is bundled and `init` already carries it as soon as `ready` arrives). Recommended push order: 1, 2, 3, 13 (baseline before anything moves), 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, then 12 and 14 and 15 as the numbers and the owner say.

## Open choice and the default taken

The drag handle, as a fixed pad at the left end of the action rail, off by default behind a setting until the owner has used it on a phone. Long-press on the space key is not buildable (the soft keyboard is the OS's and the app cannot hook its keys); edge swipes would claim touches over the editor WebView and break decision 18. The handle's accessibility actions give the non-gesture route.

## Decisions this plan proposes

- The cursor handle is a fixed pad at the left end of the action rail, outside the rail's horizontal scroll; the rail's testID moves onto the scrolling region when a leading slot is given so the harness's edge-to-edge swipes never start on the pad, and the pad ships behind the setting `leetcode-cursor-handle`, off until the owner has used it on a phone.
- A drag becomes the caret by the finger's displacement from where it grabbed, never by velocity: sideways moves characters for the first 12 characters of travel and words beyond, vertical moves lines at the base column, the axis locks after the slop, a selection at the grab makes the head move and the anchor stay, and every number lives in one DEFAULT_TUNING the phone retunes.
- A drag makes at most one `select` call in flight carrying the newest target (latest-wins, released by the reply and not by a frame timer, so nothing waits on a clock and nothing queues), at the revision read once with `getText` at the grab; a change to the text during the drag ends it.
- The motion helpers work on the editor's own text read at the grab, not on `lastWebViewTextRef` or the screen's copy, which can be a keystroke behind and lose the selection's direction and the revision (decision 29's rule for whole-document keys); lines are logical lines, so with word wrap on a wrapped line is one stop, and characters are code points, not grapheme clusters.
- The `select` step gains an optional `reveal` (the head scrolls into view at the nearest edge) because `view.reveal` centers the line, which suits E2's jump and not a drag, and a second call per frame would double the 19 to 30 ms cost; EditorInput holds `selectionChanged` during a drag and tells React once at its end.
- The handle's accessibility is an adjustable role with increment and decrement for one character and four named actions for word and line, calling the same pure code as the drag; the code has no accessibility actions today, so none had to be reconciled, and only a phone with TalkBack or VoiceOver shows them working.
- Cold start is measured by one `editor.cold_start` event whose durations come from an injected monotonic clock, a page-side `evalMs` carried on `ready`, and a byte budget on `cm6Bundle.ts` that only a decision raises; milliseconds are printed by the browser script and the Android units and judged, never asserted (owner rule 46), and the owner's phone numbers come from the mobile log source after one cold open.
- The cold-start trims run in this order and only on the numbers: the runner warm-up gate (the unconfirmed 8.4 s stalls dwarf the bundle), then lazy grammar evaluation inside the one bundle; not built: a second script for language packs, an editor WebView preloaded at the app root, a static code preview before hydration, and hydrating before the problem text arrives, because the catalog is bundled and `init` already carries the text as soon as `ready` arrives.

## Risks

- RNGH Pan on the pad is the repo's idiom (TracingCanvasSvg, ReorderableList) and the harness stub drives it from pointer events, but only a native build shows it against the workspace's tab-swipe PanResponder in ProblemWorkspace.tsx (RNGH cancels the JS responder when it activates; on web the rail is portalled to body, so the web twin cannot show that conflict).
- The harness's `scroll_until_visible` starts a LEFT swipe at the region's left edge plus 10% of its width (clis/harness-core/src/device/commands.rs:527-536); a 56 px pad inside the `from` region would turn rail scrolls into huge caret drags in editor-typing and editor-completion. Mitigation: the rail's testID moves onto the scroll region (slice 7) and slice 10 runs the Done swipe with the pad present.
- Touching a native view over the soft keyboard may drop the WebView's focus on a real Android or iOS (the existing keys call `focus()` after every press for that reason). The session calls `focus()` once at the grab; slice 11 checks the keyboard on the CI emulator, and only a phone shows iOS.
- A call costs 19 to 30 ms on the emulator, so the caret trails a fast finger by one reply; the coalescer stops it queueing, but a 5 s rpc timeout on a hung call would freeze a drag until it expires. Whether the lag feels fine is a phone judgment.
- Without the selection hold every call's `selectionChanged` would re-render ProblemEditorScreen (it is large) at the call rate; the hold means Copy and the rails lag until release, which is intended and must be seen on a phone.
- `reveal` scrolls CodeMirror's own scroller, not the page's ScrollView: with the keyboard up and an editor taller than the visible area, a caret can sit under the rail exactly as it can today when typing. Not new, not fixed here.
- The `select` step change regenerates cm6Bundle.ts; slices 4 and 15 must be pushed alone, in order, and serialized against any F1 or F3 slice that touches the editor bundle. No F2 slice touches @lib/webview-rpc, so pyodidePage.ts is never regenerated by this item. EditorInput.tsx is edited by slices 8 and 13 and ProblemEditorScreen.tsx by slice 14 only; keep them in the stated order.
- On web the pad may lose its gesture to a browser pan (`pointercancel`, see docs/gaps/wf-browser-swipe-needs-frames.md); the fix is `touch-action: none` on the pad and slice 10 is where it would show.
- Browser numbers are not phone numbers: the iframe shares the app's main thread and a desktop parses fast, the twin never calls onLoadStart, and the console echo has no time in it. The deterministic figure is the byte budget (662,693 now); the milliseconds are directional until the emulator and the owner's phone confirm them.
- Lazy grammar evaluation rests on two unproven facts (esbuild wraps a `require()`d ESM package lazily; vitest resolves `require` in this source). The slice opens with that spike and is dropped if either fails. Even when it works the bytes still cross the bridge, so the expected saving is tens of milliseconds, not seconds.
- Holding the warm-up trades editor smoothness for first-Run latency (2.8 s from start to first result on the CI emulator); it ships only with evidence, because the gap file's two explanations (the Pyodide boot and the old stale accessibility node, already fixed) are not yet told apart.
- Changing the default to on re-baselines the flow goldens that show the action rail and, later, the Android goldens from the CI bundle; golden churn is limited to slice 12 because the slot is unused until the setting is on.

## What only the phone or the owner can show

- The feel of the drag on a phone: pixels per character, where characters become words, lines per pixel, the dead zone and axis lock, and whether extending a selection when one exists (instead of collapsing it) is what a thumb expects; the starting values are guesses in DEFAULT_TUNING.
- Whether the pad at the left end of the action rail is reachable with the owner's thumb and big enough (56 by about 50 dp), and whether it should sit on the syntax rail instead.
- The soft keyboard staying up through a drag and a tap on the pad on a real Android and on an iPhone, and RNGH against the workspace's tab swipe on a native build.
- TalkBack and VoiceOver: the adjustable role, the increment and decrement actions, and the four named actions.
- The cold start as felt on a mid or low Android phone, and one cold open on the phone so the `editor.cold_start` line can be read from the mobile log source; the owner decides the keep or revert threshold for slice 15 (default 30 ms on the CI emulator) and whether trading warm-up timing for typing smoothness (slice 14) is wanted.
- Flipping the handle's default to on (slice 12), and the Android goldens that follow from the CI Android bundle.
